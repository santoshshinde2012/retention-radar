# Use-case data pack (seed 42)

_Generated by `python -m retention_radar.cli.build_use_cases`. Do not edit by hand._

One day of the renewal business for a monthly AI coding assistant plan: the subscribers whose renewal is seven days out, what the policy suggests for each, what the messaging tool actually sent, and whether each subscriber renewed. Scenario rows are real **test-split** rows (never used for training, calibration or τ); Maya and Arjun are worked examples scored at T-7. Expected results are what the committed `models/` bundle returns (τ = 0.16); `python -m retention_radar.cli.build_use_cases --check` re-verifies them. Synthetic data only, no real PII.

Stories and top drivers:

- **Maya: capped on Pro, first renewal since the cut**: Pro, 3 renewals paid, and this is her first renewal since the weekly cap was cut. 4 cap hits in 14 days, 68% of requests on the cheaper model, 2 active days this week against 15 in 28. The policy picks the limit reset over a discount: her problem is the cap, not the price. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `limit_hits_14d` +0.63, `cheap_model_share_28d` +0.30, `first_renewal_after_pricing_change` +0.21.
- **Arjun: steady Pro+, deliberately left alone**: Pro+ for 12 months, 66% of the allowance used, no cap hits, accept rate steady. Scoring him is useful; contacting him is not. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `renewals_completed` -0.53, `cheap_model_share_28d` -0.22, `accept_rate_change` -0.16.
- **First renewal, already fading**: Renewal number one: 2 active days this week, 7 in 28, 19% cheap-model share. First renewals are the cliff; the only playbook worth arming is the cancel-flow discount, paid only if they click cancel. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `renewals_completed` +0.51, `accept_rate_change` +0.28, `cheap_model_share_28d` -0.22.
- **Side-project user between projects**: 55% of activity on weekends and 0 active day(s) this week. This is the subscriber a pause offer exists for. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `accept_rate_change` +0.22, `ide_sessions_28d` +0.12, `limit_hits_14d` -0.10.
- **Switched overage off after a bill**: Paid $14.70 of overage in 28 days, then switched it off; 4 cap hits since. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `limit_hits_14d` +0.59, `cheap_model_share_28d` +0.33, `renewals_completed` -0.27.
- **Quiet week, long tenure**: 0 active day(s) this week against 5 in 28, but 10 renewals paid and no cap trouble. A dip is not a lapse. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `cheap_model_share_28d` -0.47, `renewals_completed` -0.37, `suggestion_accept_rate_28d` -0.19.
- **Eligible, but in the holdout**: P(lapse) is above τ and a playbook would pay back, but this subscriber hashes into the 10% holdout. ## Walk the whole service

```bash
make use-cases            # queue → packets → action log → outcomes + lift vs holdout
# or step by step (fresh output dir; the action import skips already-logged rows):
rm -rf artifacts/use_cases && mkdir -p artifacts/use_cases
python -m retention_radar.cli.batch_score --csv data/use_cases/daily_t7_batch.csv \
  --out artifacts/use_cases/queue.csv --scored-at 2026-09-28T06:00:00Z
python -m retention_radar.cli.single_record --dir data/use_cases/records \
  --out artifacts/use_cases/packets.jsonl
python -m retention_radar.cli.action_log --from-scores artifacts/use_cases/queue.csv \
  --decisions data/use_cases/actions_taken.csv --log artifacts/use_cases/action_log.csv
python -m retention_radar.cli.outcomes --log artifacts/use_cases/action_log.csv \
  --labels data/use_cases/renewal_outcomes.csv --out artifacts/use_cases/outcomes.csv
curl -s localhost:8000/v1/churn/score -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d @data/use_cases/records/maya_capped_pro.json
```
