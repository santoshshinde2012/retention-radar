# Use-case data pack (seed 42)

_Generated by `python -m retention_radar.cli.build_use_cases`. Do not edit by hand._

One day of the renewal business for a monthly AI coding assistant plan: the subscribers whose renewal is seven days out, what the policy suggests for each, what the messaging tool actually sent, and whether each subscriber renewed. Scenario rows are real **test-split** rows (never used for training, calibration or τ); Maya and Arjun are worked examples scored at T-7. Expected results are what the committed `models/` bundle returns (τ = 0.14); `python -m retention_radar.cli.build_use_cases --check` re-verifies them. Synthetic data only, no real PII.

Stories and top drivers:

- **Maya: capped on Pro, first renewal since the cut**: Pro, 3 renewals paid, and this is her first renewal since the weekly cap was cut. 3 cap hits in 14 days, 64% of requests on the cheaper model, 2 active days this week against 15 in 28. The policy picks the limit reset over a discount: her problem is the cap, not the price. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `cheap_model_share_28d` +0.45, `first_renewal_after_pricing_change` +0.16, `cli_sessions_28d` -0.11.

- **First renewal, already fading**: Renewal number one: 1 active days this week, 14 in 28, 27% cheap-model share. First renewals are the cliff; the only playbook worth arming is the cancel-flow discount, paid only if they click cancel. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `renewals_completed` +0.59, `ide_sessions_28d` -0.29, `first_renewal_after_pricing_change` +0.19.
- **Side-project user between projects**: 75% of activity on weekends and 1 active day(s) this week. This is the subscriber a pause offer exists for. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `renewals_completed` +0.56, `cheap_model_share_28d` -0.31, `accept_rate_change` +0.28.
- **Switched overage off after a bill**: Paid $1.97 of overage in 28 days, then switched it off; 3 cap hits since. - **Quiet week, long tenure**: 2 active day(s) this week against 15 in 28, but 10 renewals paid and no cap trouble. A dip is not a lapse. Top drivers (SHAP, log-odds): `renewals_completed` -0.44, `suggestion_accept_rate_28d` -0.31, `accept_rate_change` -0.24.
- **Eligible, but in the holdout**: P(lapse) is above τ and a playbook would pay back, but this subscriber hashes into the 10% holdout. ## Walk the whole service

```bash
make use-cases            # queue → packets → action log → outcomes + lift vs holdout
# or step by step (fresh output dir; the action import skips already-logged rows):
rm -rf artifacts/use_cases && mkdir -p artifacts/use_cases
python -m retention_radar.cli.batch_score --csv data/use_cases/daily_t7_batch.csv \
  --out artifacts/use_cases/queue.csv --scored-at 2026-09-28T06:00:00Z
python -m retention_radar.cli.single_record --dir data/use_cases/records \
  --out artifacts/use_cases/packets.jsonl
python -m retention_radar.cli.action_log --from-scores artifacts/use_cases/queue.csv \
  --decisions data/use_cases/actions_taken.csv --log artifacts/use_cases/action_log.csv
python -m retention_radar.cli.outcomes --log artifacts/use_cases/action_log.csv \
  --labels data/use_cases/renewal_outcomes.csv --out artifacts/use_cases/outcomes.csv
curl -s localhost:8000/v1/churn/score -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d @data/use_cases/records/maya_capped_pro.json
```
